The principle

Five columns of equal width, but with 1, 2, 3, 6 and 6 rows. Each column stands for a period: the further right, the older and the terser the presentation. The layout explains how an archive works without a single word.

ColumnClassArticlesPresentation
1 — current.cf-blog-col--11large title, author, date, reading time
2 — this year.cf-blog-col--22title, date, reading time
3 — last year.cf-blog-col--33title, date, reading time
4 — before that.cf-blog-col--66title only, single line
5 — archive.cf-blog-col--66title only, single line

The grid shows 18 articles in total. The counter in the section header names the full figure — the jump from 18 visible to 189 available is the invitation into the archive.

Behaviour

  • Below 900 px the grid collapses to one column and the timeline is dropped.
  • Single-line titles are truncated with text-overflow: ellipsis — the title attribute carries the full text.
  • Hover lightens the cell (white 45 %); nothing moves.
  • Every cell is an <a> — the whole area is clickable, not just the title.
The timeline is decorative. It is aria-hidden because the years already appear in each article's metadata. Without that, a screen reader announces “Aktuell 2026 2025 2024 2022–2023” with no context at all.
