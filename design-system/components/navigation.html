Behaviour

StateAppearance
Defaultfrosted glass at 32 %, links in --text-secondary
Hoverlink goes to --text-primary, 120 ms
Current pagearia-current="page" → white pill behind the link
Stickystays at the top on scroll; the frosted layer carries the contrast
Below 780 pxlinks fold under the bar, the toggle button appears
Frosted glass needs substance behind it. If a page starts without hero artwork, the navigation sits over the grey wash — that is enough. Over a white surface it disappears; those pages get --surface-glass: rgba(0,0,0,.04) and a 1 px contour instead.

Accessibility

  • The bar lives in a <nav>; with more than one navigation, each gets an aria-label.
  • The current page is marked with aria-current="page", not by colour alone.
  • The toggle carries aria-controls and aria-expanded and updates both.
  • Every page starts with a .skip-link to #inhalt.
  • Mono uppercase is read normally by screen readers — the casing happens in CSS, not in the markup.
<a class="skip-link" href="#inhalt">Zum Inhalt springen</a>
