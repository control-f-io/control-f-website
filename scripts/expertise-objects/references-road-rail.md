# Road, rail and air reference grounding

Primary-source references reviewed on 7 September 2026. Shapes are simplified
for the shared isometric composition, not drawn to vehicle scale or labelled as
exact replicas. The ship and excavator references, plus the aircraft image links, are in
[REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md).

## Truck and trailer

- [MAN TGS/TGX assembly photographs and chassis views, page 1](https://public.man.eu/media/service/asp/media/en/480067.pdf)
  were visually inspected. The photo shows the steering axle under the cab,
  a separate drive axle behind it, the fifth-wheel coupling, broad windshield,
  dark central grille, and paired headlamps.
- [MAN cab range](https://www.man.eu/mea/en/trucks/all-models/the-man-tgs/equipment/tgs-equipment.html)
  documents low/compact cab options. The drawing keeps the flat roof requested
  by the user instead of copying the high-roof variant in the assembly photo.
- [Schmitz Cargobull container chassis specification and layout](https://www.cargobull.com/fr/-/media/project/scb-sites/scb-global-site/media-center-en/brief-information-container-chassis-scf-40-lx.pdf)
  supplies the three-axle running-gear and container-support arrangement. Its
  extracted technical information supplements the MAN photographic reference.

The SVG now separates the tractor's two axles from the trailer's three, connects
the trailer through a fifth wheel, fits the container to the trailer, and gives
the front face a centered grille and symmetric headlights. It retains the
user-requested flat cab roof.

## Locomotive and intermodal wagon

- [Ferrotract's own G6 locomotive photograph](https://www.ferrotract.fr/documents/fiches-techniques/5-fiche-g1206.html)
  was visually inspected. This operator source shows three equally spaced
  axles, a central elevated cab between two low hoods, a thin projecting roof,
  access doors, and handrails along the walkways.
- [Tatravagónka Sgnss 60 intermodal wagon](https://tatravagonka.sk/wagons/sgnss-60-4/?lang=en)
  and [manufacturer drawing](https://tatravagonka.sk/inc/uploads/2020/05/Sgnss_60_e.jpg)
  ground the low container deck, two two-axle bogies, and end coupling.

The SVG uses that central-cab shunter layout, even axle spacing, hood apertures,
separate walkway rails, and paired buffers. The wagon has supported bogie side
frames and a physical coupling to the locomotive. Container proportions are
compressed to balance the fleet composition and remain legible at mobile size.

## Airbus passenger jet

- [Airbus A320neo product page](https://www.aircraft.airbus.com/en/aircraft/a320-family/a320neo)
  supplies two official photographs visually inspected for this revision: the
  front three-quarter in-flight image and the MSN6101 aircraft on the apron.
  Direct image links are recorded in [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md).
- The in-flight photograph establishes the slim fuselage, rounded nose, swept
  low wings, raised wing tips, compact cockpit windows and conventional tail.
  The apron photograph establishes the underwing nacelle attachment, broad
  circular intake lips, and separate nose and main landing gear.

The previous turboprop is replaced by an A320neo-inspired jet. Its fuselage is
one continuous loft silhouette; the cockpit panes use the same surface geometry
and are clipped to that silhouette. Wing and engine pairs share mirrored lattice
coordinates. Jet intakes replace exposed propellers and their orbit marks.
The illustration remains a simplified fleet symbol, not a scale aircraft drawing.
