# Renewable asset image references

Reviewed 7 September 2026. These manufacturer images ground the construction and
silhouettes of `erneuerbare()` in `objects.py`. The scene is a portfolio of assets
at independent illustrative scales, not a buildable plant layout or an exact
replica of the named products. Logos, product colours and photographic textures
are omitted to preserve the site's near-white isometric system.

## Wind turbine

- [Vestas EnVentus turbine product page](https://www.vestas.com/en/energy-solutions/onshore-wind-turbines/enventus-platform/v150-6-0)
- [Nacelle photograph on Vestas' image CDN](https://vestas.scene7.com/is/image/vestas/V162-7.2MW%20Prototype%2004?%24Default-Image%24=&dpr=off&qlt=85&ts=1721293808645&wid=1024)

Visually inspected the rectangular nacelle behind the rounded hub, panel seams,
roof instrumentation and circular tower sections. The drawing uses a longer
panelled nacelle, a tapered spinner, a small roof instrument and a tower access
door. Three identical blades remain spaced by exactly 120 degrees.

## Transformer

- [Hitachi Energy substation transformer](https://www.hitachienergy.com/products-and-solutions/transformers/distribution-transformers/liquid-filled-distribution-transformers/small-distribution-transformers-up-to-315-kva/substation-distribution-transformers)
- [Product photograph](https://dynamic-assets.hitachienergy.com/is/image/hitachiabbpowergrids/substation-dt-product-image-component-1200x675px%3A16-9?fit=crop%2C1&fmt=png-alpha&hei=675&wid=1200)

The photograph shows a tank on feet, an overhanging lid, a projecting radiator
bank with upper/lower connections and gauges. The drawing turns its former
surface hatching into a physical five-fin radiator bank with connected headers,
and adds a tank gauge. Its three roof bushings are a simplified connection
arrangement, not a claim about this particular photographed unit.

## Hydrogen electrolyser

- [Nel atmospheric alkaline electrolysers](https://nelhydrogen.com/product/a-series-alkaline/)
- [Nel electrolysers brochure, A485 render and process diagram, printed pages 1–3](https://nelhydrogen.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Electrolysers-Brochure_PD-0600-0125-Rev-F.pdf)

Visually inspected the long horizontal A485 filter-press cell stack with end
plates, tie rods and a steel skid; two upright separator vessels sit at one end.
The drawing replaces three generic upright cylinders with this recognizable
stack-and-separator arrangement. Cell-plate count is condensed to six visible
rings for small-screen clarity, and short pipe connections meet the stack.

## Battery energy storage

- [Sungrow PowerTitan 2.0](https://www.sungrowpower.com/us/en/products/utility-energy-storage-system/powertitan2)
- [Sungrow PowerTitan 2.0 whitepaper, product render on printed page 6](https://us.sungrowpower.com/upload/file/20240821/PowerTitan%202%20WhitePaper.pdf)

The product render shows a flush cabinet with tall service doors, small handles,
a dark base rail and a narrow top band. The SVG keeps three evenly spaced closed
door bays, removes the large window-like rectangles and adds hinges, handles,
base rails and a compact end cooling grille. Its roof is still the scene's only
gradient face and retains four equal corner nodes.

## Solar module table

- [Schletter fixed-tilt systems, FS Duo](https://www.schletter-group.com/mounting-systems/fixed-tilt-systems/)
- [FS Duo product render](https://www.schletter-group.com/strapi/uploads/fixedtilt_fsduo_sectionfix_v02_b5e53badbf.png)

Visually inspected the open structure below the modules: slender paired posts,
continuous rails and uniformly inclined framed modules. The SVG replaces the
solid supporting walls with four real posts and two rails, adds modest bracing
and an even panel subdivision. Its inclination remains snapped to the brand's
isometric drawing angles rather than copied as a product specification.

All reference files were viewed locally for comparison. They are not bundled as
website assets; only original vector geometry is shipped.
