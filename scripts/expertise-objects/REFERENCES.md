# Visual references

Manufacturer and operator images inspected on 7 September 2026 to ground the equipment
silhouettes. The SVGs are simplified original drawings in Control-F's near-white
isometric register; they do not identify a particular equipment model. Reference
photographs are not bundled or reproduced on the website.

- **Rolls-Royce / mtu generator:** [Series 4000](https://www.mtu-solutions.com/na/en/applications/power-generation/power-generation-products/diesel-generator-sets/series/4000.html),
  [official assembly photograph](https://www.mtu-solutions.com/content/dam/mtu/application/power-generation/series/4000/MTU_20V4000HD_Persp2_oeFA_FS_2021.png/_jcr_content/renditions/original./MTU_20V4000HD_Persp2_oeFA_FS_2021.png).
  Tall radiator, exposed repeated cylinder-head covers and intake canisters,
  horizontal alternator with terminal housing, connected pipework, and one low skid.
- **Container vessel:** [Damen container feeders](https://www.damen.com/vessels/cargo/container-feeders/container-feeder-900),
  [official underway photograph](https://medialibrary.damen.com/transform/b78bdb0c-45e5-446b-ada4-e2163d19f7fb/DAMEN-CONTAINER-FEEDER-800-565846-SMALAND-162-JPG?io=transform%3Afill%2Cwidth%3A3840%2Cheight%3A3840&quality=75).
  Aft accommodation and bridge, orderly container stacks, raised bow, and a hull
  meeting the water with a small wake. No keel blocks or exposed underwater hull.
- **Airbus passenger jet:** [Airbus A320neo](https://www.aircraft.airbus.com/en/aircraft/a320-family/a320neo),
  [official front three-quarter photograph](https://www.aircraft.airbus.com/sites/g/files/jlcbta126/files/styles/w780h520/public/2023-09/2016-00_a320neo_aib_in-flight_2.jpg?itok=k56D5xY6),
  [official aircraft-on-apron photograph](https://www.aircraft.airbus.com/sites/g/files/jlcbta126/files/styles/w780h520/public/2022-10/4-A320neo%20MSN6101%20with%20engine%20PW1100G%20GTF%20Advantage%20-%20First%20flight%20.jpg?itok=lHI94TQt).
  Slim rounded fuselage, swept low wings with raised tips, conventional tail,
  two underwing jet nacelles with circular intakes, nose and main landing gear,
  and compact cockpit panes inset into the rounded nose.
- **Tracked excavator:** [Liebherr official crawler-excavator announcement](https://www.liebherr.com/en-id/n/the-liebherr-r-920-crawler-excavator-for-less-regulated-markets-at-bauma-china-2016-24055-5290562),
  [official photograph](https://www-assets.liebherr.com/media/bu-media/lhbu-corp/news/corp_news_2016/corp_11_18/corp_22_14/corp_baumachina/corp_crawler_excavator_r920/liebherr-r920-crawler-excavator-tier3-02-72dpi_w800.jpg).
  Paired crawler tracks, rear counterweight, glazed cab beside the articulated
  boom, visible hydraulic linkage, and a digging bucket.
