**Anstellung:** Vollzeit, Festanstellung

## Deine Rolle

Du verantwortest den Aufbau, die Automatisierung und den sicheren Betrieb unserer Azure-basierten Datenplattform. Im Mittelpunkt stehen Infrastructure as Code mit Terraform, CI/CD in Azure DevOps und der Betrieb von Azure Databricks. Du entwickelst technische Standards und setzt sie gemeinsam mit unseren Development- und Data-Teams um.

## Deine Aufgaben

- CI/CD-Pipelines in Azure DevOps entwickeln und betreiben – einschließlich Service Connections, Artifacts, Environments und Freigabeprozessen

- Azure Databricks automatisiert provisionieren und betreiben, einschließlich Workspaces, Cluster Policies, Jobs/Workflows, Libraries und Secrets

- Reproduzierbare Deployments über Entwicklungs-, Test- und Produktionsumgebungen sicherstellen

- Monitoring und Alerting mit Azure Monitor und Log Analytics aufbauen und weiterentwickeln

- Identitäts-, Berechtigungs- und Netzwerkkonzepte umsetzen, unter anderem mit Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD), RBAC, Key Vault, VNETs und Private Endpoints

- Betriebsstörungen analysieren, Ursachen beheben und wiederkehrende Fehler durch Automatisierung vermeiden

- Fundierte Kenntnisse in Azure DevOps, insbesondere Pipelines, Repos, Service Connections und Environments

- Gute Deutsch- und Englischkenntnisse, um technische Themen in beiden Sprachen klar zu vermitteln

Control-F is a fully remote data consultancy working on projects in mechanical engineering, automotive, and energy. We are hiring a DevOps Engineer with hands-on experience in Terraform and Azure DevOps to help build and operate our Azure-based data platform.

**Location:** Remote, with an optional office in Konstanz, Germany
**Employment:** Full-time, permanent
**Start:** Immediately
**Salary:** €65,000–85,000 gross per year, depending on experience

## Your role

You will take responsibility for building, automating, and securely operating our Azure-based data platform. Your focus will be Infrastructure as Code with Terraform, CI/CD in Azure DevOps, and Azure Databricks operations. You will define technical standards and implement them together with our development and data teams.

## Your responsibilities

- Develop and operate CI/CD pipelines in Azure DevOps, including service connections, artifacts, environments, and approval processes

- Provision and manage Azure infrastructure with Terraform using reusable modules, remote state, and version-controlled changes

- Automate the provisioning and operation of Azure Databricks, including workspaces, cluster policies, jobs/workflows, libraries, and secrets

- Ensure reproducible deployments across development, test, and production environments

- Build and improve monitoring and alerting with Azure Monitor and Log Analytics

- Implement identity, access, and network controls using Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD), RBAC, Key Vault, VNETs, and Private Endpoints

- Investigate incidents, resolve root causes, and use automation to prevent recurring failures

## What you bring

- Hands-on experience with Microsoft Azure and cloud infrastructure operations

- Solid knowledge of Azure DevOps, particularly pipelines, repos, service connections, and environments

- Production experience with Terraform: modular structures, remote state, versioning, and CI/CD integration

- Scripting skills in PowerShell or Bash; Python is a plus

- An understanding of identity and access management, networking fundamentals, and least privilege

- A structured approach to work and the ability to document technical decisions clearly

- Good German and English skills, with the ability to explain technical topics clearly in both languages

### Useful, but not required

- Experience with Azure Databricks and data platform infrastructure

- Docker and Kubernetes/AKS

- Azure Landing Zones, Policy as Code, or GitOps

- Deeper knowledge of observability and tracing, for example with Application Insights or New Relic

## What we offer

- Projects involving industrial telemetry data and large data volumes

- Scope to shape architecture, automation, and operational standards

- Technical collaboration with a specialized team and short decision-making paths

- No overnight on-call duty

- Fully remote work, with an optional workspace in Konstanz, Germany

- Flexible working hours and workation options

- A training and certification budget, for example for Azure or Terraform

- 30 days of vacation and a company pension scheme

- A home-office equipment budget and team events
